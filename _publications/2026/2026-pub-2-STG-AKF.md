abstract: >-
  This work proposes STG-AKF, an adaptive UWB-IMU indoor localization framework that combines spatiotemporal graph attention modeling with an adaptive error state Kalman filter. By estimating ranging bias and uncertainty under NLOS conditions, the method improves localization accuracy, robustness, and real-time trajectory tracking in complex indoor environments.
